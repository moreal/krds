KRDS Copyright and License Information

KRDS (Korea Design System) is a design system provided by the Government of the Republic of Korea to enhance consistency and user experience in digital services.

KRDS materials (design assets, code, documentation, etc.) may be used freely for both commercial and non-commercial purposes. However, the source must be clearly credited by indicating “KRDS (Korea Design System)” or the name of the providing institution.

Terms of the KOGL Type 1 License

All KRDS materials are provided under the Korea Open Government License (KOGL) Type 1. This allows users to utilize the design and development resources included in KRDS freely, provided they comply with the conditions below.

  1. 1.Source Attribution
    • Users must clearly indicate the source of the materials when using public works.
      • Example: “This work uses the ‘Government-wide UI/UX Design System (KRDS)’ created by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety in 2024 and released under KOGL Type 1. The materials can be downloaded free of charge from the KRDS Design System website (www.krds.go.kr).”
      • When possible, include a hyperlink to the source website when providing attribution.
  2. 2.Commercial Use
    • Anyone may freely use the materials for both commercial and non-commercial purposes.
  3. 3.Permission for Modification
    • Users may modify, adapt, and use the materials, and the creation and distribution of derivative works is permitted. (Please note that the government bears no responsibility for any loss or disadvantage that may result from the use of improperly modified public materials.)
    • For more details, please refer to the KOGL website.
